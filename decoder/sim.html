MODELED How the sim works: it replays real history instead of a bell curve. For each coin it takes the last 3 years of real daily candles (close, high and low vs the prior close), cuts them into 5-day blocks, and shuffles those blocks into 50,000 new 180-day paths for BTC (10,000 × 90 days for every other coin). Two adjustments, both stated: (1) zero drift: the historical average daily return is removed (BTC's sample drifted +26%/yr; keeping it would bake a bull market into every path), so up and down start even; (2) volatility scaled to now: every move is scaled so the paths run at the average of the last 30 days' and last year's volatility (BTC: 37% and 44%, so 41% annualized). Touches use daily highs and lows, so wicks count. Stops count only on a daily close below. What it can't know: news, ETF flows, a regime change. That is why the prediction-market column sits beside it.

Because the sim has zero drift, its expected value is roughly minus the fees by design: the sim gives fair odds, it does not create an edge. An edge has to come from somewhere else (the fear entry, a catalyst, a breakout).
