Bands match alternative.me's style: 0-24 Extreme Fear, 25-44 Fear, 45-55 Neutral, 56-75 Greed, 76-100 Extreme Greed. Every input uses only data up to that day, so the backtest has no look-ahead. Funding (OKX perps, last ~30 days) is shown beside the score as a check but is not in the formula, because free history is only ~30 days and could not be backtested.

Backtest: "buy extreme fear, sell extreme greed"

The honest result: after Coinbase fees the rule beat simply holding on 8 of 20 coins that ever triggered (SOL, XRP, AVAX, SUI, DOT, NEAR, PEPE, BEAM). Those wins are mostly coins that fell over the period, where being in cash most of the time helped. On the biggest winners (BTC, BNB, LINK, QNT, DOGE) it lost badly, because "extreme greed" tends to fire early in a run and the rule sells too soon. At Uphold's ~7% round trip it is worse on every coin.

The part that does work: as an entry signal, our extreme-fear days were followed by a better-than-average 90-day median return on 16 of 20 coins with at least 30 fear days (BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, DOGE, LTC, AVAX, SUI, BNB, DOT, NEAR, TRX, ZEC, ADA, LINK, PEPE). Extreme greed was followed by a better-than-average 90-day median on 12 of 19 coins, so greed is often momentum, not a top. So: buy fear on coins where it has historically paid, and hold through greed with a trailing stop on Coinbase instead of selling the greed print.
